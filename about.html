Who We Are

Your parts-supply partner

Zayaan Investments is a dedicated supplier of heavy-duty truck, trailer and machinery spare parts, committed to helping businesses keep their vehicles and equipment operating efficiently.

We understand that commercial vehicles and heavy machinery are essential to the success of transport, construction, logistics and industrial operations. When a vehicle or machine is out of service, productivity is affected — that's why we focus on dependable parts sourcing and supply solutions tailored to our customers' specific requirements.

Our product coverage includes spare parts for a wide range of leading European, Asian and international truck brands, as well as heavy equipment, machinery and trailers.

From individual replacement parts to specific sourcing requirements, our approach is simple: understand what the customer needs, identify the correct product and provide a suitable solution based on the required specification.
